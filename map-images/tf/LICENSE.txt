Map thumbnails: Team Fortress 2 (tf)

Files:   <map>.webp for the 233 maps of the game that have a menu picture
         (every maps/<map>.bsp of the installation except background01,
         itemtest and cp_cloak, which have none, and pl_cactuscanyon and
         rd_asteroid, whose picture is a "BETA" placeholder), mostly
         128 x 96
Source:  the game's own menu pictures
         (materials/vgui/maps/menu_thumb_<map>.vtf in tf2_textures_dir.vpk;
         pl_hoodoo_final: menu_thumb_pl_hoodoo), converted to WebP with
         scripts/overviews/thumbs.ts on 2026-11-21 (npm run map-thumbs),
         the filler rows at the bottom cut off
Owner:   Valve Corporation. Team Fortress 2 and these pictures are the
         property of Valve Corporation (community maps: made for Valve's
         game by their authors).
License: not covered by gameWE's license (GPL-3.0-or-later) and not licensed
         by Valve to gameWE; included only so the banners can show which map
         is running. They are removed on request of the rights holder.
         Operators can delete this folder or put their own pictures in
         /var/lib/gamewe/web/map-images, which are used first.
